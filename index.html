Bienvenidos

An Antioquian fonda on Melrose Avenue

Step off Melrose and into a cream-walled dining room: wooden tables and chairs, brass pendant lamps, a ceiling fan turning slowly, Colombia on the walls.

The plates come the Antioquian way, generous and unhurried, from the bandeja paisa the regulars order most to a sancocho that has simmered all morning.
